From 1013 protons to a handful of anti-atoms

A Monte Carlo model of CERN's antimatter chain: proton beam → antiproton production → Antiproton Decelerator → ELENA → Penning trap → antihydrogen → magnetic trapping. Every cycle loses about twelve orders of magnitude. Change the machine and watch where the yield goes.
